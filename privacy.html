Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 4, 2026

TL;DR: We don't collect, sell, or share your personal data. All data stays on your device. We only use Better Auth (Cloudflare Workers) for authentication and SePay VietQR for payments.

1. Overview

Rank Checker ("we", "our", "the extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data when you use our Google Chrome extension.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

  • Email address: Required for account creation and authentication
  • Password: Securely hashed, never stored in plain text
  • User ID: Auto-generated unique identifier

2.2 Usage Data

  • Keywords you check: Stored locally to show in history
  • Domains you track: Saved for convenience
  • Ranking history: Results from your rank checks
  • Search preferences: Settings like Google domain, language, depth

2.3 Subscription Data

  • Plan type: Free or Pro
  • Subscription status: Active, canceled, etc.
  • Billing date: Next payment date for Pro users

3. How We Store Data

  • Local Storage: All ranking history, keywords, and preferences are stored locally in your browser using Chrome's storage API
  • Better Auth (Cloudflare Workers): Authentication data (email, PBKDF2-hashed password, session tokens) stored securely
  • No Server-Side Storage: We do NOT store your ranking data on our servers

4. How We Use Your Data

We use collected data solely to:

  • Authenticate your account and maintain login sessions
  • Track your ranking history locally
  • Remember your search preferences
  • Provide Pro features if you have an active subscription
  • Process payments via SePay VietQR

5. Payment Processing

Payment transactions are processed by SePay VietQR (Vietnamese bank transfer):

  • We do NOT store credit card or bank account information
  • Payments are made via VietQR bank transfer to our TPBank account
  • We only receive confirmation of successful transfers via SePay webhook
  • SePay Privacy Policy: https://sepay.vn/privacy

6. Data Sharing

We DO NOT:

  • ❌ Sell your data to third parties
  • ❌ Share data with advertisers
  • ❌ Track your browsing activity outside the extension
  • ❌ Collect analytics or telemetry
  • ❌ Use your data for marketing purposes

We ONLY share data with:

  • ✅ Better Auth (Cloudflare Workers): For authentication (required for login)
  • ✅ SePay: For payment processing (only if you upgrade to Pro)

7. Third-Party Services

7.1 Better Auth / Cloudflare Workers (Authentication)

  • Purpose: User authentication and session management via email verification
  • Data shared: Email, password (PBKDF2-hashed), session tokens
  • Privacy Policy: https://cloudflare.com/privacy

7.2 SePay VietQR (Payment Processing)

  • Purpose: Subscription payment processing via Vietnamese bank transfer (VietQR)
  • Data shared: Email, payment amount (no bank details stored)
  • Privacy Policy: https://sepay.vn/privacy

7.3 Google Search

  • Purpose: Performing rank checks
  • Data shared: Search queries you initiate via the extension
  • Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

8. User Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data: View all stored data in extension settings
  • Delete your account: Contact us to permanently delete your account and data
  • Export your data: Export ranking history to CSV/XLSX
  • Opt-out: Uninstall the extension to stop all data collection

9. Data Retention

  • Local history: Kept until you manually clear or uninstall extension
  • Account data: Kept while account is active
  • Deleted accounts: Data removed within 30 days

10. Security

We implement security measures to protect your data:

  • Passwords are hashed using industry-standard algorithms
  • Authentication tokens are stored securely
  • HTTPS encryption for all data transmission
  • No plain-text storage of sensitive data

11. Children's Privacy

Our extension is not intended for users under 13 years old. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the extension after changes constitutes acceptance.
